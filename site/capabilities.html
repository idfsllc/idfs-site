Our Capabilities

We are a design & fabrication firm focused on using advanced technology, robotics, and experience to bring ideas to life. We provide full service fabrication and specialize in product design, scene work, and sculptural milling.

Design & Engineering Services

Full service design, prototyping, and reverse engineering services. We take your ideas from concept to reality and leverage years of experience in engineering and manufacturing.

  • Design & Engineering in CAD/CAM
  • Prototyping
  • Reverse Engineering

Fabrication and Machining

Full Scale Fabrication and machining capabilities located in Fayetteville, Georgia. We can fabricate and machine anything from a small part to a large scale project.

  • Fabrication
  • Machining
  • Welding

Industrial 3D Printing & Additive Manufacturing

Professional-grade additive manufacturing using industrial 3D printing systems for production-quality parts, functional prototypes, and end-use components. Our facility features small and large resin bath SLA (stereolithography) printers, delivering precision, durability, and material properties suitable for manufacturing applications, aerospace, defense, and industrial use.

  • Industrial-Grade Equipment & Materials
  • Production-Quality Parts & Components
  • Engineering-Grade Plastics & Composites
  • Functional Prototyping & End-Use Manufacturing
