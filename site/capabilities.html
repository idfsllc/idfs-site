Our Capabilities

Bringing ideas to life through advanced fabrication, engineering, and modern manufacturing.

We combine technology, robotics, and hands-on experience to deliver precision fabrication, engineered design, and production-ready solutions for industries that demand reliability and accuracy.

Design & Engineering

Turning concepts into manufacturable, production-ready solutions.

We provide CAD modeling, product development, and engineering support for projects ranging from simple components to full assemblies.

Capabilities include:

  • CAD modeling (Fusion 360, SolidWorks-compatible workflows)
  • Reverse engineering
  • 2D/3D technical drawings
  • Prototyping and design validation
  • DFM/DFA optimization (design for manufacturability)
  • Fixtures, jigs, tooling, and assembly design
  • Rapid concept modeling via SLA printing

Benefits:
Shorter development cycles, reduced production cost, and clear, manufacturable designs from day one.

Metal Fabrication

Comprehensive metal fabrication with tight control over accuracy, fitment, and finish.

We build structural and cosmetic components for industrial, aerospace, automotive, and entertainment applications.

Capabilities:

  • Mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum fabrication
  • Tube, plate, channel, and structural assemblies
  • Frames, carts, enclosures, mounts, brackets
  • Precision fitment and alignment
  • Bending, drilling, tapping, and finishing
  • Hardware installation and mechanical assembly

Applications:
Equipment frames, commercial builds, industrial components, fixtures, props, prototypes, custom hardware, R&D projects.

Welding (MIG/TIG)

Certified, high-quality welds that meet structural and cosmetic requirements.

We handle everything from structural weldments to delicate aluminum and stainless.

Welding capabilities:

  • MIG welding (steel, stainless, aluminum)
  • TIG welding for thin-wall tubing and precision components
  • Chromoly and specialty alloy welding
  • Structural and architectural welds
  • Weld cleanup, grinding, and finishing

Use cases:
Load-bearing structures, precision assemblies, prototype components, commercial fabrication, and entertainment builds.

CNC Machining

Precision machining for small parts, components, and prototypes.

Our in-house machining ensures accuracy, repeatability, and high-quality finishing.

Capabilities:

  • CNC milling for metals and plastics
  • Side milling, surfacing, pocketing, and contouring
  • Drilling and tapping
  • Precision dowel fitment
  • Tolerance control within ±0.005" standard (tighter optional)

Common materials:
Aluminum (6061/7075), mild steels, stainless steels (304/316), plastics (Delrin, UHMW, ABS, acrylic), composites.

Applications:
Brackets, housings, fixtures, spacers, adapters, test parts, small production components.

SLA 3D Printing (Formlabs 3L & Industrial Resin Systems)

High-resolution, production-quality resin printing—far beyond hobby-grade capabilities.

Our Formlabs 3L system supports large-format prints with engineering-grade materials suitable for real-world use.

Build volume: 13.2 × 7.9 × 11.8 in (335 × 200 × 300 mm)
Large enough for housings, models, functional parts, fixtures, and production tools.

Material options:

  • Tough / Durable (ABS-like functional parts)
  • High-Temp Resin (tooling, molds, fixtures)
  • Rigid Resin (glass-filled stiffness)
  • Elastic / Flexible materials
  • Biocompatible medical resins
  • Draft resin for fast prototyping

Post-processing:

  • Alcohol baths
  • UV curing
  • Support removal / sanding
  • Painting and finishing
  • Hardware install / hybrid assemblies

Applications:
Tooling, functional prototypes, engineering models, medical models, molds, fixtures, industrial enclosures.

Capabilities:
