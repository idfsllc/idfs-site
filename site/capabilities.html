Metal Fabrication

Our welders have all the equipment and experience needed for structural and cosmetic welds. We handle everything from simple repairs to complex assemblies.

Fabrication processes:

  • In-house design
  • MIG & TIG welding
  • Part cutting
  • Assembly & finishing


Learn more about Metal Fabrication

CAD Design & CAM Programming

Our design team transforms concepts into detailed CAD models. We offer CAD modeling capabilities including Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Rhino, Catia, and more. Our expertise delivers precise, accurate designs ready for visualization, iteration, and fabrication.

Capabilities:

  • CAD modeling (Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Rhino, Catia, etc.)
  • CAM programming in Mastercam, RobotMaster, and Fusion360.
  • Reverse engineering & 3D scanning.
  • Design for manufacturability (DFM) and design for assembly (DFA).


Learn more about CAD design & CAM programming

Robotic / Cutting Capabilities

Robotic milling and cutting equipment for metals, plastics, composites, and specialty materials.

Equipment:

  • 4 Kuka robots
  • Robotic 7-axis milling cell with rotary table
  • CNC plasma cutter
  • Shop Sabre CNC Router
  • Horizontal bandsaw


Materials:
Aluminum, mild steels, stainless steels, plastics, composites, urethane foam, and more.


Learn more about Robotic / Cutting Capabilities

Machining

Precision CNC milling & turning machines for in-house machining and production runs.

Process capabilities:

  • Milling, surfacing, pocketing, and contouring
  • Threading
  • Custom shapes and profiles

Additive Manufacturing (Industrial 3D Printing)

High-resolution, production-quality resin printing equipment with engineering-grade materials using Formlabs 3L.

Applications:
Product development, props, scenic fabrication, functional testing, tooling, medical, veterinarian, and dental models, trays, engineering prototypes, subassemblies, and production assemblies.
