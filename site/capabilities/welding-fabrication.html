Metal fabrication

Metal fabrication for industrial, commercial, automotive, aerospace, and entertainment applications. We handle everything from complex frames and assemblies to brackets, fixtures, enclosures, and short production runs.

Fabrication processes:

  • In-house design team
  • MIG & TIG welding
  • Part cutting
  • Assembly & finishing
  • Packaging & shipping

Applications:
Frames, enclosures, trailers, brackets, assemblies, structural reinforcements, precision components, ground support equipment, and more.

MIG & TIG welding

Precision welding for major metals, engineered for strength and appearance—cosmetic, load-bearing, or structural.

Welding services:

  • MIG welding (steel, stainless, aluminum)
  • TIG welding for thin-wall tubing and precision components
  • Structural and architectural welds
  • Weld cleanup, grinding, and finishing
