CAD design & CAM programming

Our design team transforms concepts into detailed CAD models and supports product development from simple components through full assemblies.

Capabilities:

  • CAD modeling (Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Rhino, etc.)
  • CAM programming in Mastercam, RobotMaster, and Fusion 360
  • Product development and design validation
  • Research and development support
  • Reverse engineering & 3D scanning
  • Prototyping—including rapid concept modeling via SLA printing
  • Design for manufacturability (DFM) and design for assembly (DFA)

Benefits:
Shorter development cycles, reduced production cost, and clear, manufacturable designs from day one.
