CAD Design & CAM Programming

Our design team transforms concepts into detailed CAD models. We offer CAD modeling capabilities including Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Rhino, and more. Our expertise delivers precise, accurate designs ready for visualization, iteration, and fabrication.

Capabilities:

  • CAD modeling (Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Rhino, etc.)
  • CAM programming in Mastercam, RobotMaster, and Fusion360.
  • Reverse engineering & 3D scanning.
  • Design for manufacturability (DFM) and design for assembly (DFA).
