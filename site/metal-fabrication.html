Custom Metal Fabrication

Full-service metal fabrication for industrial, commercial, aerospace, and entertainment applications in Fayetteville, Georgia.

We handle everything from complex frames and assemblies to brackets, fixtures, enclosures, and made-to-order projects.

What is Custom Metal Fabrication?

Custom metal fabrication is the process of cutting, bending, welding, and assembling metal materials to create custom parts, structures, and assemblies. At IDFS, we combine traditional fabrication techniques with modern equipment to deliver precision metalwork for any application.

Our metal fabrication capabilities include:

  • Steel, stainless steel, aluminum fabrication
  • Tube and plate fabrication
  • Structural and architectural builds
  • Custom frames, mounts, racks, and carts
  • Brackets, hardware, and support components
  • Production fitment and assembly
  • Finishing and post-processing options

Materials We Work With

We fabricate a wide range of metals for diverse applications:

Steel:

  • Mild steel (A36, 1018, 1020)
  • Structural steels, tubing, plate, angle, channel

Stainless Steel:

  • 304, 316, 410 stainless
  • Food-grade and corrosion-resistant applications

Aluminum:

  • 5052, 6061, 7075 aluminum
  • Lightweight structural applications

Specialty Alloys:

  • Chromoly (4130)
  • Specialty alloys on request
