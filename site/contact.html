What to Include in Your Quote Request

Providing complete details helps us deliver an accurate quote quickly.

  • Part Drawings – Dimensions, tolerances, and any necessary notes
  • Material Specifications – Material type, grade, and special requirements
  • Quantities – Prototype, batch runs, or production volumes
  • Quality Requirements – Inspections, documentation, or special criteria
  • Timeline – Required delivery dates and critical milestones
  • Special Requirements – Packaging, finishing, or handling needs

Our Quote Process

  1. Submit RFQ
    Send your project details and files through the form or email.
  2. Internal Review
    Our design and fabrication team reviews all specifications and requirements.
  3. Receive Quote (Typically within 24 hours)
    You'll receive a detailed quote outlining scope, pricing, and scheduling.
  4. Optional Project Discussion
    We can schedule a call or meeting if your project requires clarification or engineering support.
  5. Begin Manufacturing
    Once approved, your project enters production and you'll receive updates as needed.
