Industries We Serve

Precision fabrication, engineering support, and custom manufacturing for a wide range of professional industries.

IDFS supports aerospace, industrial, automotive, defense, film, commercial, and consumer clients with accurate, reliable, and fast-turnaround fabrication services.

Aerospace

Fabrication and precision work for aerospace applications.

We support aerospace companies with components, brackets, tooling, and custom fabrication requiring accuracy, repeatability, and reliable fitment.

Typical work includes:

  • Metal components and brackets
  • Fixtures and tooling
  • Prototype hardware
  • R&D parts
  • Fabricated assemblies

Why aerospace clients work with IDFS:
Fast communication, tight tolerances, and the ability to move from idea to physical part efficiently.

Industrial & Manufacturing

Reliable fabrication and machining for industrial operations.

We produce components, replacement parts, assemblies, and support equipment for manufacturing environments.

Typical work includes:

  • Machine components
  • Mounts, brackets, and hardware
  • Replacement or upgraded parts
  • Production support items
  • Custom fabricated solutions

What industrial clients value:
Durability, consistent quality, and rapid turnaround when equipment needs support.

Automotive

Fabricated and machined components for automotive projects, prototypes, and specialty builds.

Typical work includes:

  • Brackets, mounts, and hardware
  • Prototype components
  • Small machined parts
  • Custom fabricated pieces

Why automotive clients choose IDFS:
Accurate fitment, clean fabrication, and flexible small-run manufacturing.

Defense

Fabrication for defense-adjacent applications and specialty equipment.

We provide reliable metalwork and precision components for companies operating in defense-related sectors.

Typical work includes:

  • Fabricated components
  • Small machined parts
  • Hardware, brackets, and support items
  • Prototype assemblies

Key advantage:
Professional, consistent fabrication built for demanding applications.

Film & Entertainment

Custom fabrication for Atlanta's film and television industry.

IDFS is located minutes from Trilith Studios, Tyler Perry Studios, and Atlanta Metro Studios, offering fast-turnaround metalwork and custom builds.

Typical work includes:

  • Props and scenic elements
  • Hero pieces
  • Metal components for set builds
  • Custom fabricated structures
  • Practical effects support items

Why productions choose IDFS:
Speed, communication, creative problem-solving, and proximity to major studios.

Commercial & Consumer

Versatile fabrication and machining for small businesses and personal projects.

Typical work includes:

  • Custom metalwork
  • Small-batch production
  • Product prototypes
  • Hardware and brackets
  • One-off custom builds

Why consumers choose IDFS:
Straightforward communication, fast service, and quality work from a professional fabrication shop.
