Design, Product Development, & Prototyping

Turning concepts into manufacturable, production-ready solutions.

We provide CAD modeling, product development, and design support for projects ranging from simple components to full assemblies.

Capabilities include:

  • CAD modeling (Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Rhino, etc.)
  • Product development
  • Reverse engineering
  • Research and development
  • Prototyping and design validation
  • DFM/DFA optimization (design for manufacturability)
  • Rapid concept modeling via SLA printing


Benefits:
Shorter development cycles, reduced production cost, and clear, manufacturable designs from day one.

Custom Metal Fabrication (Welding, Machining, Cutting)

Metal fabrication for industrial, commercial, automotive, aerospace, and entertainment applications. We handle everything from complex frames and assemblies to brackets, fixtures, enclosures, and short Production runs.

Metal fabrication process:

  • In-house design team
  • MIG & TIG welding
  • Part cutting
  • Assembly & finishing
  • Packaging & shipping


Applications:
Custom metal fabrication, frames, enclosures, trailers, brackets, assemblies, structural reinforcements, precision components, ground support equipment, and more.

Additive Manufacturing (Industrial 3D Printing)

Large-format SLA printing services for production-quality parts, prototypes, and functional components using Formlabs 3L.

3D printing services:

  • Functional prototypes and parts
  • Complex geometries not feasible in machining
  • Wide range of materials and applications
  • Design and engineering support


Applications:
Product development, functional testing, tooling, medical, veterinarian, and dental models, trays, engineering prototypes, subassemblies, and production assemblies.


Learn more about Additive Manufacturing (Industrial 3D Printing)

Part Cutting (Plasma, Router, Robotic Milling, Bandsaw)

We offer a wide range of cutting services for metals, plastics, composites, using a variety of tools and machines.

Cutting services:

  • CNC plasma cutting
  • Shop Sabre CNC Router
  • 7-Axis robotic aluminum milling cell w/ rotary table
  • Horizontal bandsaw


Use cases:
Base plates, gussets, brackets, signage, component blanks, templates, subassemblies, and production parts.
