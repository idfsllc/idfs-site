Our Services

Your full-service design, fabrication, and manufacturing partner in Fayetteville, Georgia.

From one-off prototypes to production runs, IDFS delivers precision metalwork, engineering support, and advanced manufacturing solutions for every industry.

Custom Metal Fabrication

Full-service metal fabrication for industrial, commercial, aerospace, and entertainment applications.
We handle everything from complex frames and assemblies to brackets, fixtures, enclosures, and made-to-order projects.

Capabilities include:

  • Steel, stainless steel, aluminum
  • Tube and plate fabrication
  • Structural and architectural builds
  • Custom frames, mounts, racks, and carts
  • Brackets, hardware, and support components
  • Production fitment and assembly
  • Finishing and post-processing options

Why it matters:
You get high-quality, professional fabrication built to withstand real-world use—delivered on time with consistent accuracy.

Welding (MIG/TIG)

Precision welding for all major metals, engineered for strength and appearance.
Our welds are clean, consistent, and structurally sound, whether cosmetic, load-bearing, or aerospace-grade.

We weld:

  • Mild steel
  • Stainless steel
  • Aluminum
  • Chromoly and thin-wall tubing
  • Specialty alloys

Applications:
Frames, enclosures, trailers, brackets, assemblies, structural reinforcements, precision components.

Cutting Services (Plasma, Saw, Waterjet)

Accurate cutting for metals, plastics, composites, and specialty materials.

Cutting capabilities:

  • CNC plasma cutting for steel and aluminum
  • Horizontal bandsaw cutting
  • Precision waterjet cutting (optional, on request)
  • Custom shapes, profiles, and nested parts
  • Clean edges and tight dimensional accuracy

Use cases:
Base plates, gussets, brackets, signage, component blanks, templates, and production parts.

CNC Machining for Small Parts

High-precision CNC milling for prototypes, small parts, and production components.

Capabilities include:

  • ±0.005" tolerance standard (tighter available on request)
  • Aluminum, steel, stainless, plastics, composites
  • Drilling, tapping, facing, contouring
  • Jigs, fixtures, spacers, mounts, brackets
  • Small-batch production runs

Benefits:
Accurate, repeatable parts—machined in-house for full quality control.

Trailer & Gate Repair

Professional repair services for residential, commercial, and industrial equipment.

Services include:

  • Gate hinge repair or replacement
  • Structural reinforcement
  • Trailer frame and tongue repair
  • Axle, fender, and mount repair
  • Welding and fabrication for broken or worn components

Why choose IDFS:
Fast turnaround, precise repairs, and durable results built to last.

Production Runs (Low–Medium Volume)

Reliable production manufacturing for businesses that need consistent quality and repeatability.

We support:

  • Prototype-to-production transitions
  • Short-run manufacturing
  • Batch parts and components
  • Hardware, fixtures, and small mechanical assemblies
  • Fitment and post-processing

Ideal for:
Startups, product developers, small manufacturers, and companies needing consistent on-time supply.

Industrial Additive Manufacturing (SLA 3D Printing — Formlabs 3L)

Large-format SLA printing for production-quality parts, prototypes, and functional components.

Capabilities:

  • Formlabs 3L industrial resin system
  • Smooth surfaces and tight tolerances
  • Functional prototypes and end-use parts
  • Jigs, fixtures, models, housings, and components
  • Complex geometries not feasible in machining

Material options:

  • Durable and Tough resins (ABS-like)
  • High-Temperature resin
  • Rigid and fiber-reinforced resins
  • Flexible and elastic resins
  • Biocompatible medical-grade materials

Applications:
Product development, functional testing, tooling, medical models, engineering prototypes, and production assemblies.

Film & Entertainment Prop Fabrication

Custom props, set pieces, and hero builds for Atlanta's rapidly growing film and TV industry.

Capabilities include:

  • Metal props, scenic elements, hero props
  • Practical effects and mechanical components
  • Structural pieces and rig-ready builds
  • Aluminum and steel scenic work
  • Fast turnaround for tight production schedules

Why productions choose IDFS:
Minutes from Trilith, Tyler Perry, and Atlanta Metro Studios—making us a reliable, local fabrication partner for high-speed builds.
