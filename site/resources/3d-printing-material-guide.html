3D Printing Material Guide

SLA resin materials for industrial 3D printing. Formlabs materials, properties, and applications.

Material Options

Our Formlabs 3L system supports a wide range of engineering-grade materials suitable for real-world use.

Material options:

  • Tough / Durable (ABS-like functional parts)
  • High-Temp Resin (tooling, molds, fixtures)
  • Rigid Resin (glass-filled stiffness)
  • Elastic / Flexible materials
  • Biocompatible medical resins
  • Draft resin for fast prototyping

Applications:

  • Tooling and fixtures
  • Functional prototypes
  • Engineering models
  • Medical models
  • Molds and production tools
  • Industrial enclosures
