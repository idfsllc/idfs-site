Industrial 3D Printing (SLA)

Large-format resin 3D printing with Formlabs 3L for production-quality parts

What is SLA/Additive Manufacturing

Stereolithography (SLA) is a 3D printing process that uses a laser to cure liquid resin into solid parts layer by layer. This technology produces high-resolution, smooth-surface parts suitable for functional prototypes, tooling, and end-use production components.

Build Volume

The Formlabs 3L offers a large build volume for printing bigger parts in a single print job.

Materials / Engineering Resins

We work with a wide range of engineering-grade resins including biocompatible materials for medical applications, high-temperature resins, and durable materials for functional parts.

Industries Served

Our 3D printing services serve aerospace, medical, industrial, automotive, and product development industries.
